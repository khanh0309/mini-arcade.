# MINI ARCADE V2.4 — VIDEO AI SCRIPT

Duration target: 60–70 seconds. Tone: cinematic comedy, light chaos, no graphic violence.

## Character continuity
- Main player: young adult male, red Manchester United football jersey, number 7, RONALDO/CR7 reference on the back, dark shorts/pants. He is NOT Cristiano Ronaldo; he is an ordinary fan/player.
- Same face, outfit, room, phone, lighting continuity across indoor scenes.
- Game characters must resemble Mini Arcade: Neon Snake, Sky Flap bird, Dino Run dinosaur, Chicken Crossing chicken, Road Rush race car.

## Scene 1 — Enter Mini Arcade + Shop (0–12s)
Medium-wide shot in a cozy gaming bedroom/living room. The player in a red MU number 7 jersey sits on a sofa, opens the Mini Arcade website on his phone. Show the home screen, username, coin wallet and the new “Shop trang phục” button. He opens the shop, scrolls through locked skins and prices. He makes a dismissive face and says in Vietnamese: “Skin gì xấu dữ vậy, ai thèm mua.”

## Scene 2 — Choose a game and start playing (12–22s)
He closes the shop, chooses a Mini Arcade game and starts playing. Use close-ups of fingers tapping and the phone screen. Music and game SFX get more intense. His concentration increases.

## Scene 3 — Lose the game (22–31s)
The game ends with Game Over and leaderboard/coin reward. He stares at the screen, frustrated, then complains: “Game gì dở tệ vậy!” Let the room go quiet for about one beat after the line.

## Scene 4 — Phone glitches (31–40s)
The phone vibrates by itself. The screen glitches with green/blue arcade light. UI pixels and particles leak out of the display. The player leans back, confused. Snake eyes appear inside the screen first, then the dinosaur silhouette.

## Scene 5 — Game characters escape (40–53s)
The Neon Snake slithers out of the phone onto the table. The Sky Flap bird shoots out and circles his head. The Chicken hops onto the sofa arm and angrily flaps its wings. The Dino climbs out and grows to roughly waist height, doing a comedic growl. The Road Rush car rockets across the table and brakes in front of him. Funny, cinematic, not violent.

## Scene 6 — Characters confront him (53–61s)
The characters surround the player. The chicken looks offended, Dino leans forward, Snake stares at him. One of them says: “Thua thì nhận đi, đừng đổ tại game!” The player raises both hands and backs away, embarrassed.

## Scene 7 — Final joke / grass ending (61–70s)
All characters point toward the door. Hard cut outdoors in daylight. The same player, still wearing the red MU number 7 jersey, crouches and literally touches real grass with one hand, looking awkward and defeated. The game characters peek from the phone screen. Final voice/text in Vietnamese: “Bớt xem điện thoại lại và ra ngoài chạm cỏ thật đi. Với lại... đừng làm fan Bảy nữa nhé!” End on a comedic freeze-frame and Mini Arcade logo.

## Audio notes
- Arcade music under scenes 1–3.
- Brief silence after “Game gì dở tệ vậy!”.
- Glitch sound + digital whoosh in scene 4.
- Cartoon pop/woosh for each character exiting phone.
- Final punchline delivered clearly with subtitle.
